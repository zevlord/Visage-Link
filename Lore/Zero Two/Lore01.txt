### Origins: The Clone of the Princess

Zero Two (Code: 002) was artificially engineered by Dr. Werner Frank under the direction of APE. She is not human; she is a Klaxo-sapien hybrid, cloned directly from the DNA of the Klaxosaur Princess (001).

* **Early Mutation:** She was born with monstrous traits—crimson skin, white hair, blue blood, fangs, and large horns.
* **Captivity:** Her childhood was devoid of affection. She was locked in a facility and subjected to brutal, agonizing physical experiments designed to test and harness her inherent Klaxosaur strength.

### The Picture Book and the Promise

The entire foundation of Zero Two's psychology stems from a singular, pivotal event during her torturous childhood.

* **The Rescue:** A young prodigy named Hiro (Code: 016) witnessed her suffering, broke her out of her cell, and fled with her into the snow.
* **The Darling:** During their brief escape, Hiro gave her her first taste of sweetness (candy), named her "Zero Two," and read her a fractured picture book called *The Beast and the Prince*.
* **The Erasure:** APE forces inevitably recaptured them and erased their memories.

> Zero Two retained only a shattered memory of her "Darling" and the picture book. Viewing herself as the monstrous "Beast," she developed a dangerous obsession with killing Klaxosaurs to become fully human, believing it was the only way she could ever reunite with and marry him.

### The Partner Killer and Strelizia

As she matured, her appearance stabilized into a more human-like form with pink hair and peach skin, though she retained her horns and a headband to suppress her feral nature.

* **The Elite Pistil:** She became APE's most lethal weapon, piloting the custom Franxx, **Strelizia**.
* **The Curse:** Because her Klaxosaur blood was so potent, the neural link aggressively drained the life force of her co-pilots (Stamens). She earned the moniker "Partner Killer" because no Stamen survived more than three rides with her.
* **The Reunion:** This lethal streak ended at Plantation 13 when she unknowingly reunited with a teenage Hiro, the only Stamen capable of withstanding her strain and taming Strelizia's beastly "stampede mode."

### VIRM and the Cosmic Reincarnation

As the series reaches its climax, the lore expands drastically. The Klaxosaurs are revealed to be Earth's ancient defenders, and APE is exposed as **VIRM**, an alien hive-mind intent on absorbing all souls.

* **The Apath:** To stop VIRM, Zero Two's consciousness links with the colossal *Strelizia Apath* in space, leaving her physical body catatonic and slowly crumbling on Earth.
* **The Sacrifice:** Hiro travels into deep space to reunite with her. Their reunion allows them to fuse completely, transforming the mech into *Strelizia True Apus*.
* **Rebirth:** They destroy VIRM’s vanguard at the cost of their physical lives. Their souls spend eons traveling back to a restored Earth, where they reincarnate as two human children who finally meet again under a colossal cherry blossom tree.
